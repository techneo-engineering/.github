# Techneo Engineering
### The Engineering Studio

We are a software engineering studio that helps agencies and growing companies fix, build and ship software that works.

Most teams don't need another vendor who starts from scratch. They need someone who can look at what they have, find what's wrong, and fix it. That's how we work.

## What we do

**Diagnose and fix**
We audit your existing codebase, infrastructure or product, find the real bottlenecks (performance, reliability, technical debt, stalled releases), and fix them.

**Project delivery**
Well-scoped web, mobile and backend projects, built to be maintained after we hand them over.

**Staff augmentation**
Engineers who slot into your team and work in your tools and processes, for as long as you need them.

## Working with agencies

We work as a white-label or partner team for agencies that need dependable engineering capacity without growing headcount. You keep the client relationship, and we handle the build and the fixes.

- Clear scope and written estimates before any work starts
- Regular updates through your channels (Slack, email, your project tool)
- Clean, documented code that your team can take over at any time

## How we work

1. **Discover:** a short call to understand the problem and the constraints.
2. **Diagnose:** we review the code, systems and requirements, and share findings.
3. **Propose:** a clear scope, timeline and price.
4. **Deliver:** iterative builds and fixes, with visible progress.
5. **Hand over:** documentation and support so nothing depends on us.
